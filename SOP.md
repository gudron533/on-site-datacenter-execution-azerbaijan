# SOP — Controlled On-Site Execution Principles

This SOP defines how on-site work is executed to minimize operational and compliance risk.

## 1. Scope control
- Execute only confirmed tasks
- No scope expansion without explicit approval
- If instructions conflict with reality: pause and escalate

## 2. No improvisation
- No “creative fixes”
- If unexpected conditions appear: document → notify → wait for decision

## 3. Evidence & reporting
- Use timestamped photo/video evidence when requested
- Provide structured updates: what was done, what was observed, what is next
- Reporting must be suitable for enterprise ticketing systems

## 4. Communication
- Technical English, concise updates
- Use an agreed channel: ticket, email, chat, bridge call
- Confirm every critical step before proceeding if risk exists

## 5. Safety & access compliance
- Follow facility rules
- Escort requirements are respected
- Chain-of-custody is maintained for parts and removed hardware
